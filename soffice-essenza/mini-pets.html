Where it comes from

Small things that make a big room feel softer.

Mini Pets started as an experiment — could a candle be a tiny sculpture, too? We began shaping little animals out of soy wax: a curled-up cat, a round hedgehog, a bunny with its ears tucked in. Each one took dozens of tries to get the proportions just right — round, soft, a little wobbly, and unbearably cute.

Now they sit on shelves and bedside tables across Ireland — loved as much for how they look as for how they smell. Sweet, cosy scents like vanilla cream and toasted marshmallow finish the feeling: small joys, hand-made, one tiny pet at a time.

Meet the collection
