Our story

Made by hand, one pour at a time — from our little corner of Ireland.

Soffice means soft in Italian — and that's the feeling we chase in everything we make. Every candle starts as melted soy wax on the stove, a hand-blended scent, and a mould we've grown to love. No factories, no rush — just small batches, poured, cooled and finished by hand.

We design around three little worlds: the hush of the sea, the playfulness of tiny creatures, and the stillness of a mindful pause. Different moods, same gentle palette — warm beige and soft pastels, chosen so each candle looks as good resting on a shelf as it does lit.

How to order

Simple, personal, made for you

We keep things small and personal — every order is hand-poured after you reach out, so no two days in the studio look the same.

01

Browse a collection

Explore Mare, Mini Pets or Namaste and find the scents and shapes that call to you. Mix and match across collections — they're made to live together.

02

Send your order

Message us on Instagram or by email with what you'd like, your address and any little notes — gift wrap, a card, a name for someone special.

03

Hand-poured & shipped

Your candles are poured, cured and packed with care, then posted across Ireland — usually within a few days of your order.

Ready to bring a little softness home?
