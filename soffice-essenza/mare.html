Collection 01 · Mare

Bottled ocean air,
poured into calm.

Mare — Italian for sea — is inspired by Ireland's wild Atlantic coast: salt-kissed mornings, sun-bleached driftwood, and the quiet hush of waves at dusk. Each candle is hand-poured in soft seafoam and sandy beige tones, so the feeling of the coast lingers long after the flame goes out.

Where it comes from

A love letter to the Irish coastline.

Mare began with long walks along windswept beaches — collecting driftwood, listening to the tide pull back over the stones, watching fog roll in over the cliffs before breakfast. We wanted to bottle that feeling: unhurried, briny, endlessly calming.

Every candle in this collection is poured in soft seafoam greens, misty blues and sandy beige — colours pulled straight from a low-tide morning. Light one, and the room slowly fills with salt air, warm driftwood and the kind of quiet that only the sea can offer.

The scents

Four ways to bring the coast indoors

Each candle is hand-poured in small batches using soy wax and skin-safe fragrance oils — clean burning, generously scented, finished in a reusable sandy-beige glass.

①

Sea Salt & Driftwood

Mineral sea salt, sun-warmed driftwood and a breath of soft musk — the smell of a quiet beach at low tide, just after the rain.

Top notes: salt, citrus & cedar
②

Atlantic Mist

Cool linen, sea spray and a whisper of bergamot — like morning fog rolling slowly over the cliffs before the sun breaks through.

Top notes: marine air, linen & bergamot
③

Linen & Foam

Clean cotton, white florals and gentle marine notes — soft and airy, like sea foam settling quietly along the shoreline.

Top notes: cotton, jasmine & sea moss
④

Coastal Dawn

Bergamot, white tea and warm amber — the first soft light over a calm harbour, just as the boats begin to stir.

Top notes: bergamot, white tea & amber
